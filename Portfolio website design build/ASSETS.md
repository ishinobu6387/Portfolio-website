## Notes

- Framing (crop, zoom, rotation, frame position) is baked into `Portfolio.dc.html` as `BAKED_CROPS` and `BAKED_LAYOUT`, keyed by slot. Index proportions are `BAKED_TOC`.
- Plan viewer sheets are cut so all levels share one registration window; re-cut replacements the same way or the levels will drift when cycled.
- Original camera and export files are in the attached `sem2projectfiles` folder; full portfolio page scans are in `uploads/`.
- Every file here is loaded by the site. 110 superseded duplicates from earlier drafts were removed, so nothing in `assets/` is dead weight.
- After ENTER the site preloads everything behind a progress screen, so paging through spreads never waits on the network. Five loading-screen treatments are available in Tweaks (`loadingScreen`).

## Resolution policy — 300ppi on screen

Every drawing is capped at **300ppi at its printed-sheet size**: 300ppi against a CSS pixel is 3.125x the size the image occupies on a 1920-wide screen, so a drawing placed 30% across a sheet needs 1800px and no more. Anything above that is invisible detail carried at full download cost.

**Image loading rule:** every `<img>` in the template uses `data-src`, and `paintPages()` promotes any `img[data-src]` to `src` after each render. Keep `ref="{{ setSrc }}"` on new images too, but the sweep in `paintPages()` is what guarantees nothing renders blank — do not remove it.

## Scaling to more projects

Total imagery is about 92MB. Preloading everything works at three projects; at eight it will not. The fix is not lower resolution - it is loading less at once:

1. **Preload per project, not per site.** Gate on splash + covers + the project being opened. Each project stays a short wait instead of one long one, and the total stops mattering.
2. **Keep the 300ppi cap as a rule for new work.** Export each drawing at 3.125x its placed size on the sheet. This is the single biggest lever and costs nothing visually.
3. **JPEG for anything photographic, PNG only for line art on white.** A model photo is 4-6x smaller as quality-90 JPEG.
4. **Archive the print masters outside the site.** Full-resolution originals belong in `sem2projectfiles`, not in `assets/` - the site only needs the screen copy.
